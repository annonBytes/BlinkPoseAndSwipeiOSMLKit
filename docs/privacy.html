Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026

PageTurn ("the app") is published by Beinmonyu Daniel Ockiya ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: we do not collect your personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers of ours that receive your information.

Camera

The app can use the front camera so you can turn pages with a wink, a head tilt, or a mouth movement. Camera images are analysed live on your device to detect the gesture. They are not recorded, not saved, and not sent anywhere. Camera access is optional; tap, swipe, and pedal page turning work without it. You can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings.

Microphone

If you choose "Follow My Playing" in Performance mode, the app listens through the microphone to work out where you are in a piece so it can turn pages at the right moment. Sound is analysed live on your device. It is not recorded, not saved, and not sent anywhere. Microphone access is optional and is only used while that feature is running. You can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings.

Scanning and photos

If you scan sheet music with the camera or choose photos to turn into a PDF, the pages are converted into a PDF stored on your device. The app only sees the photos you select; it does not access the rest of your photo library. Scans and photos are not sent anywhere.

Your music files

PDF scores and MIDI files you add, and any annotations you draw, are stored on your device inside the app's storage. We cannot see them. They may be included in your device backups (for example iCloud Backup) according to your own iOS settings.

Settings and usage on your device

Your gesture settings, chosen page-turn modality, page-turn timings recorded for a piece, and the start date of your free trial are stored locally on your device. This information never leaves your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details or Apple ID. Apple tells the app only whether you have an active subscription. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or advertising identifiers. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not sell or share personal data, because we do not have any.

Third parties

The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting software.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. You control everything the app stores by deleting scores inside the app or deleting the app itself, which removes its data from your device.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above. If a future version of the app collects any information, we will describe it here and in the App Store privacy details before that version is released.

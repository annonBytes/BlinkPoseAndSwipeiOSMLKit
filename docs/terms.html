What the app does

PageTurn lets you view your own PDF sheet music and turn pages hands-free using taps, swipes, a Bluetooth page-turner pedal, or camera-based gestures. It also offers annotation, a metronome, and MIDI-timed automatic page turning.

Using gestures safely

Camera gestures and automatic page turning depend on lighting, camera position, your movements, and your device, and are not perfectly reliable. For important performances, rehearse with the settings you plan to use and keep a manual method such as a tap, swipe, or pedal available. Do not use the app while doing anything that requires your full attention, such as driving.
